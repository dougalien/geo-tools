App guide

The Rock Cycle

Explore how rocks change from one type to another, at your own pace, with photos of real lab samples. No grades and no quizzes.

Open The Rock Cycle (opens in a new tab)

Get started

  1. Open the app. The menu on the left lists the pages.
  2. The Big Picture shows the whole cycle and what drives it.
  3. Rock Gallery introduces 17 common rocks with photos and what to look for in lab.
  4. Take a Journey lets you become a rock and choose your own path through the cycle.
  5. Rock Detective takes what you see in a sample and suggests candidates. Glossary has quick definitions.

Tips

  • Start with Take a Journey if you like stories, or the Gallery if you like to look first.
  • Use it alongside lecture and lab: look up the rocks you handled, then follow one on a journey.
  • Photo credits and licenses are under each photo.
  • If nobody has used the app for a while it goes to sleep. Click the button that says the app is asleep (“Yes, get this app back up”) and give it up to a minute.
