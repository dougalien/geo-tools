App guide

Quake

Explore recent earthquakes from the U.S. Geological Survey, inspect any event, and talk it through with a guided earthquake science mentor.

Open Quake (opens in a new tab)

Get started

  1. In the sidebar on the left (on a phone, tap the arrow at the top left to open it), keep Realtime feed and choose a time window and magnitude, or pick Custom catalog search for your own dates, magnitudes and map box.
  2. Click Load earthquake data.
  3. Read the map and the time pattern, then choose one event from the event list to inspect it.
  4. Click Start guided exploration to talk through what you see with the mentor.
  5. Download the event table if you want to work with the numbers yourself.

Tips

  • The mentor asks questions instead of handing over answers. If the AI is busy, a built-in guide takes over, so the page always works.
  • Data: USGS ComCat earthquake catalog, public domain.
  • If nobody has used the app for a while it goes to sleep. Click the button that says the app is asleep (“Yes, get this app back up”) and give it up to a minute.

Quake is free to use for teaching and learning. Made by Dougalien Systems, part of Earth Systems Arcade.
