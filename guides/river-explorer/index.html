App guide

River Explorer

Type a river, a town, a storm you remember or a USGS site number. The app finds the real stream gage and shows its hydrograph, so you can read how the river behaves.

Open River Explorer (opens in a new tab)

Get started

  1. Type into What do you want to explore?, or open the gallery of river personalities and pick one (or click Surprise me).
  2. Choose Explore this gage on the result you want.
  3. Look at the shape of the hydrograph first. Then turn on Compare with normal to see how today ranks against other years.
  4. Try the extras: compare with a second gage, road salt (specific conductance) and base flow recession.
  5. Click Start guided exploration to talk through what you see. The session report collects your work for your instructor.

Tips

  • The guide asks questions so you find the answer yourself.
  • If your class uses the AI helper, your instructor may give you a passcode.
  • Data comes live from the USGS Water Data API. If the app says USGS is busy, it tells you how long to wait.
  • If nobody has used the app for a while it goes to sleep. Click the button that says the app is asleep (“Yes, get this app back up”) and give it up to a minute.
