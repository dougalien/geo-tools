App guide

Spectrum Earth

Fit any landform profile, such as a valley, a volcano or a river long profile, with perfect shapes and measure how far it departs from them. It is Wadell’s grain-shape idea applied to landforms.

Open Spectrum Earth (opens in a new tab)

Get started

  1. The app opens on Explore the library: real transects across 10 valleys, 10 volcanoes and 10 mid-ocean ridges, already fitted.
  2. Pick an Ideal shape at the top to compare against.
  3. Switch to Fit a profile to load your own CSV, TXT or Excel file, or one landform from the library.
  4. Set the Dataset Type, click Apply Preset, and say what the feature is (valley, hill, long profile or ridge).
  5. Read the fit and the departure on Ideal Shape and Shape Chart.

Tips

  • The departure does not depend on units, so a 200 m valley and a 5 km volcano compare on the same scale.
  • Use Crop X Range to trim a profile to one feature before fitting.
  • Elevation data: Copernicus DEM GLO-30 on land and ETOPO1 at sea.
  • If nobody has used the app for a while it goes to sleep. Click the button that says the app is asleep (“Yes, get this app back up”) and give it up to a minute.

Spectrum Earth is free to use for teaching and learning. Made by Dougalien Systems, part of Earth Systems Arcade.
