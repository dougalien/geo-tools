Game guide

Lithonaut

A 3D field trip through the rock cycle. Walk three field sites, break off hand samples and identify 15 igneous, sedimentary and metamorphic rocks.

Open Lithonaut (opens in a new tab)

Get started

  1. Open the game and choose Start exploring. A mouse and keyboard work best.
  2. On the field map, pick a site: Ash Hollow (igneous), Cutbank Creek (sedimentary) or Schist Ridge (metamorphic).
  3. Press 1 to 5 to walk to a rock, then hold E (or the mouse button) to take a sample.
  4. Look closely with the hand lens and name the rock. Your field notebook keeps track.
  5. Finish all three sites to open Erratic Moraine, where the rock families are mixed, then the Trophy Hall.

Tips

  • Turn on voice narration (V) to hear every page read aloud. Microsoft Edge has the most natural voices.
  • In settings, switch Hold E to sample to Press E once if holding a key is hard.
  • Choose Graphics: Fast on an older laptop.
  • In the hand lens, the arrow keys move the view and + and − change magnification.
  • The game is a large file, so the first load can take a few seconds.
