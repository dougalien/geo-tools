Game guide

Solar System Adventure

Fly from the Sun to Pluto with PEBBLES, your ship computer. At each of 21 stops, use four instruments to gather facts, then decide whether anything could live there.

Open Solar System Adventure (opens in a new tab)

Get started

  1. Open the game and choose Start here.
  2. On the flight plan, pick a stop. Earth is a good first stop to calibrate your instruments.
  3. Press 1 to 4 to walk to each instrument and answer its question.
  4. When all four readings are in, press 5 to judge NASA’s three ingredients for life and rate the world.
  5. Your answers are compared with NASA’s pages. Check the mission log and awards as you go.

Tips

  • Points: 10 for a question right on the first try, 5 after a miss. The life assessment is worth up to 30.
  • Turn on voice narration (V) and set the reading speed in Settings.
  • Settings also has mouse look speed and a Fast graphics mode for older laptops.
  • Press Tab on the flight plan to move between stops with the keyboard.
