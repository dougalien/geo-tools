App guide

Hydrotherm

Compare the water a data center uses for cooling with the extra evapotranspiration caused by climate warming, with every number checkable by hand.

Open Hydrotherm (opens in a new tab)

Get started

  1. Start on tab 1 · Data center: set the IT load, utilization and PUE, and pick how to estimate water.
  2. On 2 · Evapotranspiration, set a basin or aquifer area and a change in ET.
  3. 3 · Water stress comparison puts both on the same units and scale.
  4. Go further with 4 · Climate attribution, 5 · Fleet scale-up and 6 · Reservoir mixing.
  5. 7 · Methods & sources lists every source.

Tips

  • Every panel has a Hand calculation section showing the equation with your numbers, so you can check it with a calculator.
  • The sidebar sets the water temperature used for latent heat across all tabs.
  • If nobody has used the app for a while it goes to sleep. Click the button that says the app is asleep (“Yes, get this app back up”) and give it up to a minute.
