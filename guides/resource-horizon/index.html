App guide

Resource Horizon

Will a resource last 100 years? Pick nickel, methane, rare earths, water or dozens more and compare flat, steady and compounding use, with a source for every number.

Open Resource Horizon (opens in a new tab)

Get started

  1. In the sidebar under 1 · Resource, type or pick a resource. For water, choose the world or a country.
  2. Read the plain-language answer and the charts on Check a resource.
  3. Under 2 · Assumptions, change how many years ahead, the growth rate, and whether to compare with reserves or identified resources.
  4. Open Compare all to see how many years each supply would last.
  5. Method and sources shows where every number comes from.

Tips

  • Open Show the math to check a result by hand.
  • Treat answers as an order of magnitude, not a forecast. Reserves change with price, technology and discovery.
  • If nobody has used the app for a while it goes to sleep. Click the button that says the app is asleep (“Yes, get this app back up”) and give it up to a minute.
