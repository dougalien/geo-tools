App guide

The Perfect Society

Answer 20 questions about how people should live together, then see which of 21 great political thinkers would recognize your society as their own.

Open The Perfect Society (opens in a new tab)

Get started

  1. Choose your thinkers. Tick at least two, or pick a group such as The social contract or Modern justice. Your results are split among the thinkers you pick.
  2. Answer the questions, one per screen. Tick This one matters a lot to me on any question you feel strongly about; it counts double.
  3. Press Analysis to see your percentage match with each thinker, the quotes behind it, and your society’s charter.
  4. Use Explore the timeline to see when each idea entered history.

Tips

  • Teachers: use For teachers: share this set with your class on the start screen. It builds a link that starts students with only the thinkers you chose.
  • Each thinker’s position links to the chapter or passage where they say it, with free full texts wherever one exists.
  • There are no right answers. The discussion questions at the end work well for pairs or small groups.
  • Written for grades 10 and 11, and open to anyone.
