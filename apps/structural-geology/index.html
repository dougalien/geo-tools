Igneous intrusions

When each body intrudes decides what it cuts and what cuts it: faults younger than an intrusion carry and offset it. Pluton roofs are domed, so lower the land surface to unroof one. The gray halo around a pluton is its contact aureole (hornfels).

Thrusts follow a flat on the décollement, then a ramp to the surface; the hanging wall folds as it rides over the ramp (fault-bend folding). Slip dies out along strike, so faults end within the map and folds plunge. This is a kinematic teaching model, not a mechanical simulation.
